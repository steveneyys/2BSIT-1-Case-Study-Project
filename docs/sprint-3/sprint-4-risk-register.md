## Top three to watch

1. **R1 and R2** (late build, late migrations): both happened in Sprints 2 and 3 and both are cheap to prevent with the D2 and D3 checkpoints.
2. **R3** (evidence before build): the final sign-off depends on it.
3. **R4** (personal data in reports): the only risk that could expose real salaries; the rule already exists and needs a test per report.
