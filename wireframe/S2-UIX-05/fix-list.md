## Severity Guide

- High: Blocks an important workflow or exposes a restricted action/data.
- Medium: Causes confusion, mistakes, or difficult recovery.
- Low: Minor wording, alignment, or presentation issue.

## Fix Status

Use:
- Open
- In progress
- Ready for recheck
- Resolved

Mark an issue Resolved only after checking the corrected behavior.
