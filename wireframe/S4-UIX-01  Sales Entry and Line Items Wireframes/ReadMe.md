# S4-UIX-01 — Sales Entry and Line Items Wireframes

## Task
Create wireframes for sales entry and sale line items.

## Screens
- Sales list with search
- New Sale entry
- Sale details and line items

## Required Design Details
- The New Sale form includes an auto-numbered transaction number.
- Customer and salesperson are selected from dropdowns.
- Users can add, edit, and delete sale line items.
- Each line item includes a product and quantity.
- Unit price is filled from the product's price history based on the sale date.
