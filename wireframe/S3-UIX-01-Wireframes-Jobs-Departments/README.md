# S3-UIX-01 — Wireframes: Jobs and Departments

**Purpose:** Ipakita ang Jobs at Departments screens sa HR module bago ang hi-fi design.

**Screens:** Admin list, granted-HR User view, Add/Edit forms, blocked-delete state, at restore state.

**Important behavior:** Admin/Superadmin may Active, Inactive, and All views with Status and Stamp. Users see active records only and actions shown depend on their assigned rights. Job and Department codes are required, unique, and read-only after creation.

**Guide reference:** Hope ERP Student Guide, §§1.2, 2.3, 3.5.
